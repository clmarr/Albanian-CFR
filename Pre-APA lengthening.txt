Attributed to an "early" Slavic loan in wˈɑrɪbɑ by Orel 1998: 514, but this stem does not appear in Derksen 2008, which only has var-i- "to heat (vb.)" and varʊ "heat". Demiraj 1997: 424 attributes this and vorbull "whirlpool" to a PIE wērb{ʰ}- but this root is unknown.
